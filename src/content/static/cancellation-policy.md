---
title: Cancellation policy
lastUpdated: '2026-05-12'
---

- **More than 60 days before check-in:** full refund less a CAD $75 processing fee.
- **30–60 days before check-in:** 50% refund.
- **Fewer than 30 days before check-in:** non-refundable. We recommend travel insurance for any trip booked inside this window.
- **Force majeure** (wildfire evacuation order, natural disaster, government-mandated closure): full refund or rebooking, no fee.
- **Host-side cancellations:** full refund issued within 48 hours.

This is the default policy shipped with the template. The operator may override these terms — confirm the active policy in your booking confirmation email.
