**What we collect.** When you submit a booking inquiry: your name, email, phone (optional), preferred dates, guest count, and any message you write.

**Why.** To respond to your inquiry. That's it.

**Who sees it.** The host, and the booking-management system the host uses. We do not sell guest data. Inquiries are used only to respond to your booking.

**Third-party services on this page.** OpenStreetMap (map tiles — only loaded when you click the interactive map) and Walk Score (walkability score — US-based service). When their content loads on this page, those services see your IP address. The "On The Radar" section fetches event data from a small backend the host operates.

**Storage technologies.** This site uses your browser's `localStorage` to cache the "On The Radar" feed so it loads instantly on your next visit. **No cookies. No tracking. No analytics by default.**

**Your rights.** You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time. Email the host directly using the contact details in the footer.

**Cross-border note.** Walk Score is a service operated from the United States. Loading the Walk Score badge means your IP address is briefly visible to a US-based service. If you'd prefer not to load it, leave the page before the badge renders.

**Updates.** This notice is current as of the date shown above. Material changes will be reflected here with a new date.
